How the score works. Each place scores 0 to 1 on five criteria, then those are combined using your slider weights. Monet: allowed 1.0, restricted 0.55, murky 0.4, banned 0. Visa comfort, diversity and English are the research scores of 1 to 5 rescaled to 0 to 1. Cost is the band inverted, so cheaper scores higher. The coloured bar in each row shows what each criterion contributed.

What is missing. Detailed monthly budgets, climate figures and design job markets were still being researched when this page was generated, so cost and weather here are coarse bands rather than numbers. Rows marked "screened" have no in-depth research behind them yet.

Trust these numbers only as a starting point. Immigration and breed rules changed repeatedly during 2025 and 2026. Verify anything you would act on, especially Monet's status, against the primary source linked in each row before making a decision.
